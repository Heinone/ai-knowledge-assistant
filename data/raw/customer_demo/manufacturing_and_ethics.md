# Aster & Loom Manufacturing and Ethical Production

## Manufacturing Philosophy

Aster & Loom believes transparency should extend beyond materials.

Every product has a documented manufacturing journey from raw materials to finished garment.

## Primary Manufacturing Partner

Factory:
Bandung Apparel Manufacturing

Location:
Bandung, Indonesia

Production:
- Shirts
- Hoodies
- Everyday trousers

## Current Core Collection Manufacturing

All Aster & Loom current core collection garments are produced by Bandung Apparel Manufacturing in Bandung, Indonesia.

This includes:
- Alpine Heavyweight Hoodie
- Meridian Everyday Shirt
- Solen Linen Shirt
- Vale Linen Overshirt
- Ridge Utility Pants

If a product page does not repeat the manufacturing location, the current core collection manufacturing rule still applies.

## Manufacturing Standards

Our production partners are required to maintain:

- Safe working conditions
- Fair employment practices
- Regular quality inspections
- Responsible waste management
- Compliance with local labor regulations

## Worker Standards

Aster & Loom requires manufacturing partners to:

- Provide safe workplaces
- Follow legal employment requirements
- Maintain transparent wage practices
- Prevent forced labor
- Provide reasonable working conditions

## Quality Control

Products go through:

1. Material inspection
2. Production quality checks
3. Final garment inspection
4. Packaging review
