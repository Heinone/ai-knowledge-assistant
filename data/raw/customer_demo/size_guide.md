# Aster & Loom Size Guide

## General Fit Philosophy

Aster & Loom products are designed with a relaxed, everyday fit.

Customers between sizes should usually size up for a roomier fit or size down for a cleaner, closer fit.

## Men's and Unisex Tops

General top size guidance:

XS:
Recommended chest: 84-89 cm
Typical height range: 160-170 cm

S:
Recommended chest: 90-95 cm
Typical height range: 165-175 cm

M:
Recommended chest: 96-101 cm
Typical height range: 170-182 cm

L:
Recommended chest: 102-109 cm
Typical height range: 178-188 cm

XL:
Recommended chest: 110-117 cm
Typical height range: 183-193 cm

XXL:
Recommended chest: 118-126 cm
Typical height range: 188-198 cm

## Overshirts

Overshirts are designed to be worn over a T-shirt or lightweight knit.

For a regular relaxed fit:
Choose your usual shirt size.

For a noticeably oversized or baggy fit:
Size up one size from your usual shirt size.

For a cleaner fit:
Size down only if you are between sizes and prefer less room.

## Vale Linen Overshirt Fit

The Vale Linen Overshirt has a relaxed layering fit.

Recommended sizing:
- Customers around 170-178 cm usually choose S or M depending on preferred fit.
- Customers around 179-186 cm usually choose M or L depending on preferred fit.
- Customers around 187-193 cm usually choose L or XL depending on preferred fit.

For a customer who is 183 cm tall and 84 kg:
- M may fit as a regular relaxed overshirt.
- L is recommended for a baggier, more oversized fit.

## Solen Linen Shirt Fit

The Solen Linen Shirt has a lighter summer fit.

Recommended sizing:
- Choose your usual shirt size for a relaxed fit.
- Size up if you prefer extra room through the chest and shoulders.
- Size down only if you prefer a closer fit.

## Ridge Utility Pants

The Ridge Utility Pants use waist sizing.

General guidance:
- Choose your usual waist size.
- Size up if you are between sizes or prefer more room through the thigh.
- The pants are designed for movement and travel, not a slim tailored fit.

## Customer Questions

"I'm 183 cm tall and 84 kg. What size should I get in the Vale Linen Overshirt?"
Answer:
M is the starting size. Choose M for a regular relaxed fit or L for a baggier, more oversized fit.

"I'm 183 cm tall and 84 kg and I like a baggy fit. What size should I get?"
Answer:
For a baggy fit, L is recommended.

"I'm 175 cm and 72 kg. What size should I get in a shirt?"
Answer:
S or M is the likely starting range. Choose M if you prefer a relaxed fit, or S if you prefer a closer fit.

## Height and Weight Fit Guidance

This guidance is approximate. Body shape, shoulder width, chest size, and personal fit preference can change the best size.

When a customer provides height and weight but not chest measurements, use this as a starting point.

For tops and overshirts:

160-170 cm and 55-68 kg:
Recommended starting size: XS or S

168-176 cm and 65-76 kg:
Recommended starting size: S or M

174-183 cm and 74-86 kg:
Recommended starting size: M

180-188 cm and 82-94 kg:
Recommended starting size: L

186-195 cm and 92-108 kg:
Recommended starting size: XL

193-200 cm and 105-122 kg:
Recommended starting size: XXL

Fit adjustments:
- For a baggy or oversized fit, size up one size.
- For a cleaner fit, stay with the starting size or size down only if between sizes.
- For layering over a hoodie or thick knit, size up one size.
- For linen shirts, choose the lighter fit unless the customer asks for oversized.
- For overshirts, choose the roomier fit because they are designed for layering.

Example:
A customer who is 183 cm and 84 kg has a starting size of M.
For the Vale Linen Overshirt:
- Choose M for a regular relaxed fit.
- Choose L for a baggier or more oversized fit.
