# Aster & Loom Returns Policy

## Return Window

Customers may return eligible products within 30 days of receiving their order.

## Eligible Returns

Products must be:

- Unworn
- Unwashed
- In original condition
- Returned with original packaging when possible

## Non-Eligible Returns

The following are not eligible for standard returns:

- Items showing signs of wear
- Items washed after purchase
- Items damaged through improper use

## Defective Products

If a product has a manufacturing defect, customers should contact support.

Examples:
- Faulty stitching
- Broken zipper due to manufacturing issue
- Manufacturing defects in fabric

## Refund Processing

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method.

Refund timing:
5-10 business days depending on payment provider.

## Exchange Policy

Customers may request exchanges when replacement inventory is available.

## Customer Questions

"Can I return a shirt after wearing it?"
Answer:
Used or washed items are generally not eligible for standard returns unless there is a manufacturing defect.

"How long do I have to return an item?"
Answer:
Eligible items can be returned within 30 days of receiving the order.
