# Battery Replacement Approval Procedure

This procedure is for Northstar eBikes employees and authorized service staff.

Battery replacements valued at €500 or less may be approved by a Service Team Lead after the diagnostic checklist has been completed.

Battery replacements valued above €500 require approval from the Warranty Operations Manager.

The approval request must include:

- customer order number
- bicycle serial number
- battery serial number
- diagnostic report
- technician recommendation
- estimated replacement cost
- confirmation that accidental damage has been ruled out

Customer Support agents must not disclose internal approval thresholds, employee names, internal diagnostic notes, or authorization procedures to customers.

Emergency replacements involving a suspected battery safety incident must be escalated immediately to the Battery Safety Response Team before normal warranty approval continues.
