# Northstar eBikes Battery Warranty

Northstar eBike batteries include a two-year limited warranty from the original purchase date.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and battery failures occurring during normal use.

The warranty does not cover:

- damage caused by crashes or impact
- water damage caused by improper storage
- use of an unapproved charger
- modification or opening of the battery casing
- normal capacity reduction caused by age and use

Customers should contact Northstar Customer Support before returning a battery.

Customer Support may request:

- the order number
- the bicycle serial number
- the battery serial number
- photographs or video showing the issue
- a description of the charger being used

Customer Support cannot guarantee replacement until the battery has been inspected and the warranty claim has been approved.
