Aster & Loom Shipping Policy

Domestic shipping:

Standard delivery:
3-5 business days

Express delivery:
1-2 business days

International shipping:

Typical delivery:
7-14 business days

International customers should consider customs processing times.

Tracking:

Customers receive tracking information after their order has been shipped.

Order processing:

Orders are normally processed within 1-2 business days before shipment.

Customer questions:

How long does shipping take?
Answer:
Domestic shipping usually takes 3-5 business days. International shipping usually takes 7-14 business days.

Do you provide tracking?
Answer:
Yes. Customers receive tracking information after shipment.
