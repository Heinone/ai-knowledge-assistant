# Aster & Loom Materials and Sourcing

## Our Material Philosophy

Aster & Loom selects materials based on quality, durability, environmental responsibility, and supplier transparency.

We believe customers should know where their clothing comes from.

## Organic Cotton

Origin:
Sri Lanka

Material:
Certified organic cotton

Supplier standards:
- Responsible farming practices
- Reduced chemical usage
- Improved soil management
- Transparent supplier documentation

Why we use organic cotton:
Organic cotton provides a soft, durable fabric while supporting more responsible farming practices.

Used in:
- Alpine Heavyweight Hoodie
- Meridian Everyday Shirt

## Cotton Quality

Aster & Loom prioritizes:
- Long-staple cotton fibers
- Stronger yarn construction
- Soft texture
- Improved durability after repeated washing

## Material Questions

Customers often ask:

"Where does your cotton come from?"
Answer:
Our organic cotton is sourced from certified suppliers in Sri Lanka.

"Why do you use organic cotton?"
Answer:
We choose organic cotton because it provides high-quality fabric while supporting more responsible agricultural practices.

"Are your materials synthetic?"
Answer:
Our core collections focus primarily on natural fibers. Any blended materials are clearly listed on each product page.
