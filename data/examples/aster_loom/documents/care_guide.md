# Aster & Loom Care Guide

## General Care Philosophy

Aster & Loom garments are designed to last when cared for properly.

General recommendations:
- Wash less often when possible
- Wash with similar colours
- Use mild detergent
- Avoid bleach
- Avoid high heat drying
- Air dry when possible

## Organic Cotton Care

Used in:
- Alpine Heavyweight Hoodie
- Meridian Everyday Shirt

Care instructions:
- Machine wash cold
- Wash inside out
- Use mild detergent
- Do not bleach
- Air dry recommended
- Tumble dry low only if needed

Why:
Cold washing and air drying help preserve fabric softness, shape, and colour.

## Linen Care

Used in:
- Solen Linen Shirt
- Vale Linen Overshirt

Care instructions:
- Machine wash cold on a gentle cycle
- Use mild detergent
- Do not bleach
- Hang dry recommended
- Steam or iron on low to medium heat if desired
- Natural wrinkling is expected with linen

Why:
Linen becomes softer with wear and washing. Light wrinkling is part of the natural texture.

## Overshirt Care

Used in:
- Vale Linen Overshirt

Care instructions:
- Wash cold on a gentle cycle
- Close buttons before washing
- Hang dry
- Avoid tumble drying to reduce shrinkage risk
- Steam lightly if needed

## Hoodie Care

Used in:
- Alpine Heavyweight Hoodie

Care instructions:
- Machine wash cold
- Wash inside out
- Avoid bleach
- Air dry recommended
- Avoid high heat drying to protect the heavyweight cotton structure

## Customer Questions

"How should I wash the Vale Linen Overshirt?"
Answer:
Wash cold on a gentle cycle, use mild detergent, close the buttons before washing, and hang dry. Natural wrinkling is expected with linen.

"Can I tumble dry the Alpine Heavyweight Hoodie?"
Answer:
Air drying is recommended. Tumble dry low only if needed, and avoid high heat.

"Will linen wrinkle?"
Answer:
Yes. Natural wrinkling is expected with linen and is part of the fabric’s texture.
