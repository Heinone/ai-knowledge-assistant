# Production Incident Escalation Procedure

This procedure defines how Vertex Systems handles production incidents.

## Severity 1

A Severity 1 incident includes:

- complete production outage
- confirmed customer data exposure
- widespread authentication failure
- critical security compromise
- data loss affecting multiple customers

Severity 1 incidents must be escalated immediately to Engineering Operations.

The on-call engineer must create an incident channel and assign an Incident Commander.

Engineering leadership must be notified within 15 minutes.

Customer-facing communication must be coordinated with the Customer Operations team.

## Severity 2

A Severity 2 incident includes significant degradation where the service remains partially available.

Severity 2 incidents must be acknowledged by the on-call engineer within 15 minutes.

If the incident is not stabilized within 30 minutes, it must be escalated to Engineering Operations.

## Incident records

Every Severity 1 and Severity 2 incident requires an incident report.

The report must include:

- timeline
- customer impact
- root cause
- mitigation
- corrective actions
- responsible owners

Severity 1 incidents require a post-incident review within five business days.
