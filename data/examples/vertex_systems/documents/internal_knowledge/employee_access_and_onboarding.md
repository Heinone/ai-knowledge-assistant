# Employee Access and Onboarding

This procedure applies to all Vertex Systems employees and contractors.

## Account creation

New employee accounts must be requested by the hiring manager at least two business days before the employee's start date.

The request must include:

- employee name
- team
- manager
- start date
- required systems
- requested repository access

The Internal Helpdesk creates the employee's core account and assigns standard access based on team membership.

## Privileged access

Administrative or production access is not granted automatically.

Production access requires:

1. approval from the employee's Engineering Manager
2. completion of the production-access training
3. multi-factor authentication
4. approval from Engineering Operations

Shared administrator accounts are prohibited.

## Access reviews

Team managers must review privileged access every 90 days.

Access that is no longer required must be removed within one business day.

## Offboarding

When employment ends, the employee's manager must notify the Internal Helpdesk before the employee's final working day.

Core account access must be disabled no later than the end of the employee's final working day.
